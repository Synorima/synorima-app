## Introduction

**SYNORIMA** is an upcoming business management platform designed to bring essential business operations together in one intelligent, connected ecosystem.

Our mission is to help businesses simplify daily operations, reduce fragmentation between applications, automate repetitive processes, and make better decisions.

Rather than relying on multiple disconnected tools, businesses should be able to manage their operations through a unified, intuitive, and extensible platform.

**One Business. One System.**

## Planned Capabilities

SYNORIMA is being designed around a modular architecture, with capabilities planned across several business areas.

### Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

- Customer and contact management
- Leads and opportunities
- Customer requests and service workflows
- Quotations and follow-ups
- Communication history
- Customer self-service portal

### Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP)

- Products and services
- Sales and order management
- Business operations
- Financial management workflows
- Business reporting

### Invoicing and Payments

- Invoicing workflows
- Expense and payment tracking
- Payment gateway integrations
- Transaction management
- Accounting-related integrations

### Communication and Automation

- Email and notification workflows
- VoIP integration
- Automated reminders
- Workflow orchestration
- Event-driven integrations

### Artificial Intelligence

- AI-assisted workflows
- Intelligent business insights
- Task automation
- AI agents and extensible integrations

### Modular Ecosystem

- Independent business modules
- Configurable workflows
- API-first integration capabilities
- Industry-specific extensions
- Scalable architecture

> **Development notice:** These are planned capabilities and architectural objectives. Their availability will depend on development progress and future releases.

## Who Is SYNORIMA For?

SYNORIMA is being designed for organizations that want to manage their operations through a unified digital environment.

Our initial focus includes:

- Freelancers and independent professionals
- Small and medium-sized businesses
- Service providers and agencies
- Retail and e-commerce businesses
- Organizations with specialized workflows

Future industry-specific modules may support sectors such as real estate and insurance.

## Our Vision

We envision a future where business software works as one connected system instead of a collection of isolated applications.

A future where technology simplifies operations, improves productivity, and enables sustainable business growth.

Our ambition is to make powerful business management technology accessible, adaptable, and easier to use.

## Our Principles

### Simplicity by Design

Complex business processes should be manageable through clear, intuitive interfaces.

### Modularity and Flexibility

Businesses should be able to use the capabilities they need and expand as their requirements evolve.

### Security and Privacy

Security, responsible data handling, and privacy should be considered throughout the product lifecycle.

### Accessibility

We aim to design accessible digital experiences, using WCAG 2.2 AA as a target and combining automated checks with manual accessibility testing.

### Interoperability

Modern business software should connect with other systems through well-designed interfaces and integrations.

### Sustainable Development

We value maintainable code, automated testing, clear documentation, and long-term architectural quality.

## Development Roadmap

SYNORIMA is currently in its planning and development phase.

Our high-level priorities are:

1. Establish the core platform architecture.
2. Develop foundational identity, access, and customer management capabilities.
3. Introduce modular CRM and operational workflows.
4. Expand into financial management, integrations, and automation.
5. Develop advanced AI-powered capabilities and industry-specific modules.

This roadmap is indicative and may evolve as the project progresses.
